## Secret Histories - Project Overview

Core Identity: Victorian-era cosmic horror FPS roguelite with stealth/combat elements. Single-player initially, co-op planned. Free culture (GPLv3).

Primary Gameplay Loop: Enter procedurally-generated underworld → explore → survive through tactical decision-making → die → learn → repeat. Death is education, not failure.

Design Philosophy: Systems over scripts. Every interaction emerges from believable physics and AI behaviors. The darkness is an active character that learns and adapts.

Key Pillars:
- Exploration: Environmental storytelling through evidence left behind
- Stealth: Light/sound management as core mechanics
- Combat: Weighty, consequential encounters with period-appropriate weapons
- Resource Management: Limited ammo, light sources, consumables
- Knowledge Progression: Player skill increases through understanding systems

Setting: 1894 Greenwich, England. Cosmic horror without tentacles. Beautiful decay aesthetic. Church ruins hiding underground crypts with SCP-inspired elements.

AI Philosophy: Enemies have goals, memories, and dynamic threat assessments. They react intelligently to player actions and environmental changes. Horror through uncertainty and powerlessness.

Technical Approach: Custom behavior trees, procedural level generation with runtime navmesh creation. Physics-based interactions drive emergent gameplay.

Progression Structure: Five-act story arc where each act adds complexity without adding new systems. Knowledge carries between runs, not gear.

Horror Implementation: Focus on atmosphere, environmental storytelling, and player imagination. Rare jump scares, emphasis on dread and helplessness.

Multiplayer Vision: Co-op that maintains tension through shared vulnerability and resource scarcity.

Modular Design: System built to support other authors creating adventures in OSR D&D and other fantasy/sci-fi genres. Base grid unit is 1.5m^2 (~5ft).

Key Systems to Remember:
- Light as resource and threat
- Sound propagation for stealth
- Physics-based interactions
- AI with persistent memory
- Procedural generation maintaining hand-crafted feel
- Victorian-era weapon handling (Receiver-style complexity option)
- Environmental storytelling through evidence
